# PodList Mac UI grade · Round 20 · Codex

## Frame

- **Task** — Find an Episode through a Subscription, saved View, or Search, then queue, play, or resume it in a Playlist and recover from edits as needed.
- **Target** — Native macOS app, SwiftUI and AppKit, macOS 26+, at `faa47e0`.
- **Era** — macOS 27.2 (26B123), minimum OS and SDK 26.0, current build with no compatibility opt-out. The app selected Light and Dark separately. Show Borders was on for the dedicated captures. Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast were off in the captures. The Retina VM desktop was 1920 × 1200 pt at 2×.
- **Posture** — Sovereign personal library with a compact companion Now Playing window.
- **Audience** — Perpetual intermediate, with a path from a first Subscription to saved filters, several queues, and repeatable playback.
- **Nearest exemplar** — Things for regular personal work, persistent selection, and recoverable edits. Apple Podcasts 27.2 is the side-by-side platform reference.
- **Character** — Blue Multicolor accent and abbreviated artwork tiles, compact two-line Episode rows with searchable saved Views, named Playlists with separate History and Listening Timeline, a selection-driven Inspector, and a floating player. The accent, pane policy, and floating player are fixed owner choices. Baseline captures are 02/03 at the default size and 35/36 at minimum, in both appearances.

**Owner decisions** — The app offers System, Light, and Dark. Blue applies as AccentColor under Multicolor, including the deliberately deep Dark selection and the corresponding system-drawn Settings label. The player stays at the bottom, glass stays on controls and scrolling sheet footers, and the toolbar stays fixed with the Playlist actions grouped. The sidebar and Inspector alternate below about 950 pt, with no automatic sidebar reappearance. At wide widths the Inspector follows selection unless explicitly turned off. Its native initial width is about 270 pt and the app records divider changes. Row, toolbar, and Inspector Play actions are deliberate. The Saved Views menu name is deliberate. Podcast-page Unsubscribe arms on the first click, disarms on other interaction, and executes immediately from menus, with Undo in both cases. Undo names a restored View or empty Playlist without navigating to it. The status row offers current-screen history and the Edit menu offers global history. Mark All Played lives in the Podcast and context menus. Delete Playlist keeps Settings open with disabled fields, Restore Playlist, and Close. Timeline Rewind appears on eligible selected rows and has menu, context, and accessibility equivalents. The sidebar and New Playlist timing misses are accepted. Spotlight writes are release/tour-only, Shortcuts donations are release-only, App Intents are on, and MCP is off by default. These choices are graded where applicable and excluded from the next-step target.

## Checklist

Coverage: **90.8%** (pass 124 · fail 14 · N/O 14 · N/A 17).

### Blockers

- **[FLOW-03] A late inline Create View save can wipe new input.** In Search (15), Create View opens an editable name field. `SearchViewCreation.swift:21–28,39–50` leaves the input and Cancel active while saving, then clears both naming draft keys unconditionally. `MacWindow.swift:535` navigates on success and `LibraryModel.swift:516–526` clears the search, including query text typed after submit. `LibraryEditingModel.swift:163–174` awaits the write and refresh. This is a source-established failure with the captured start state. The Entry Undo path in 44 preserves input as intended. **Fix** — Capture the submitted draft revision and the window's Search context. On success clear only that revision. If the name, query, or route changed, keep the current edit and offer Open View in the status row and Saved Views menu.
- **[DATA-01] Close discards an unfinished Playlist Settings draft.** The deleted sheet in 11a contains the disabled “Morning Commute” name and says the edits come back on restore. In-sheet Restore and ⌘Z return the draft. Close, Return, and Esc route to `cancel()`, which deletes the stored draft. The tour exercises Close → Trash → Restore → reopen and verifies “Commute” in the field. The Close label does not communicate discard. [PlaylistSettingsViews.swift](../../apps/apple/Sources/PodListUI/PlaylistSettingsViews.swift#L142) · [Draft storage](../../apps/apple/Sources/PodListUI/SheetSettingsDrafts.swift#L142) · [Tour assertions](../../apps/apple/UITour/PodListUITour.swift#L928). **Fix** — Route deleted-state Close, Return, and Esc through a single dismissal that preserves the staged draft for the Playlist ID. Keep explicit Cancel as discard on the editable sheet.
- **[MENU-02] Show Restored Entries has no menu-bar command.** State 44 says where the result went and provides a button that deliberately changes the query, selection, and scroll to reveal it. The button is wired through `EditingFeedback` and `MacWindow`, with no corresponding case in `MenuCommandActions` or the Edit/Playlist command definitions. `menus.txt` corroborates the absence from the command index. Undo and Redo perform different actions. [EpisodeEditingViews.swift](../../apps/apple/Sources/PodListUI/EpisodeEditingViews.swift#L271) · [MacWindow.swift](../../apps/apple/Sources/PodListUI/MacWindow.swift#L336) · [MenuCommandActions.swift](../../apps/apple/Sources/PodListUI/MenuCommandActions.swift#L7). **Fix** — Publish the same window-local handler and enabled predicate as Edit › Show Restored Entries, in a stable position and dimmed while unavailable.

### Majors

- **[RESP-01] Inspector selection and opening Settings miss 200 ms p75.** In the Light/Dark timings the Inspector opens in 408.1/554.5 ms, updates in 327.0/679.1 ms, and Settings opens in 398.8/972.3 ms. `MacWindow.swift:413–432` and `ConnectionViews.swift:55–86` mark completed content. These operations have no separately measured quicker acknowledgment in the bundle. The sidebar and New Playlist misses in the timing table are owner-accepted. **Fix** — Time row-selection paint, Inspector shell paint, fresh details, and Settings window paint separately. Prioritize immediate selected state and a rendered Settings shell, with Inspector notes and pane content ready independently of that first response. Verify five-run p75 ≤ 200 ms for the first visible acknowledgment or result.
- **[TOOL-02] Playlist Search overflows before lower-priority items at half display.** In 39 the 960 pt Commute toolbar retains the disabled Show Inspector and More Episode Actions plus the active Playlist Settings. The Search field from 06, 10, 37, and 38 is absent and “more toolbar items” occupies the trailing edge. `MacWindow.swift:372–410` places the section selector, command group, Inspector, and Search in the same primary-action run. ⌘F opens full Library Search, a different scope. **Fix** — Give the trailing search a compact, visible in-list Search button at this width or protect the field's space before overflow. Keep the owner's Playlist command grouping and move the currently disabled Inspector out of the visible run first.
- **[CTRL-03] Toolbar Search loses its identifying text on entry.** The 15 and 44 fields show only the magnifier, typed value, and clear button in the toolbar. `MacWindow.swift:1027–1037` supplies an empty visual label and puts the name in the prompt. Its accessibility label survives. The inline View-name field entered from 15 uses its label the same way (`SearchViewCreation.swift:23–27`). **Fix** — Use a persistent Search identification beside or inside the field and a visible Name label on the inline form.
- **[CTRL-07] Search scope becomes implicit while typing.** The persistent labels in XCUI are “Search Library” in 15 and “Search this list” in 44. The visible control loses both qualifiers when the binding is nonempty (`MacWindow.swift:408–410,1027–1037`). The title “Commute” names the open Playlist and leaves the scope to inference. **Fix** — Keep “Library”, “This List”, or “Episodes” visible beside the value, using the same scope caption for the compact toolbar route.
- **[DRAG-06] A filtered destination drops its selection follow-up.** The 44 frame establishes that filtering an Entries table is supported. An accepted transfer reserves rows, reports per-slot progress, and commits, then `WindowModel.revealDrop` removes the pending IDs **before** guarding for empty Search. With an active filter the commit provides neither selected results nor a deferred reveal action. [EpisodeTransfer.swift](../../apps/apple/Sources/PodListUI/EpisodeTransfer.swift#L79) · [LibraryModel.swift](../../apps/apple/Sources/PodListUI/LibraryModel.swift#L393). **Fix** — Select the transferred IDs that match the current query. Keep the others pending and offer Show Added Entries using the same opt-in reveal pattern as 44.
- **[APPEAR-03] Show Borders leaves a custom player action without a visible boundary.** Compare regular and `borders-light/03` and `borders-dark/03`. The group outline and position track respond, while Open Active Playlist remains a plain icon plus name/count without a button edge. `PlayerViews.swift:98–109` uses a custom label with `.buttonStyle(.plain)`. `MacGlass.swift:24–38` provides the opaque Increase Contrast/Reduce Transparency fallback for the group, which does not outline this target. **Fix** — Use a native style that draws a button edge under Show Borders or give this target an explicit edge in that accessibility mode. Check both appearances together with each fallback.
- **[APPEAR-04] The Dark Settings tab label is below the system-drawn accent floor.** Sampling the interior text and adjacent pill in 27 and 30 at 2× gives General `#435FDD` on `#484747` at **1.73:1**, and Automation `#435FDD` on `#4A4947` at **1.68:1**. Both are below the Dark platform floor of 2.5:1 and the 4.5:1 small-text target. In Light the same sample is `#2146BE` on `#FDFDFD`, **7.69:1**. Selected Episode title pixels in 03 are white on `#193CAD` in Light (**9.17:1**) and on `#1C35A6` in Dark (**9.96:1**). White title text on sampled Dark player backdrop pixels in 36 is at least **9.72:1** for the sampled interior pixels, including where content passes below the bar. Armed Unsubscribe samples in 20 are **5.48:1** Light and **6.51:1** Dark. The player sample is specific to the sampled scroll state and artwork. [Accent asset](../../apps/apple/Resources/MacAssets.xcassets/AccentColor.colorset/Contents.json) · [Settings TabView](../../apps/apple/Sources/PodListUI/ConnectionViews.swift#L55). **Fix if the platform or owner opens this choice** — Use an independent semantic foreground on the native selected Settings label that reaches at least 2.5:1 on the observed Dark pill and preferably 4.5:1, with the selection fill keeping its own accessible role. The system currently draws this pair and the owner accepts it. This is excluded from the next step.
