## Frame

- **Task:** find Episodes through Views, Search and podcast pages, put them into Playlists (Play Now / Next / Last), and listen from the player bar, with every edit undoable.
- **Target:** Mac app (SwiftUI, macOS 26+).
- **Posture:** sovereign (an all-day listening and library tool), with a transient Now Playing sheet.
- **Audience:** perpetual intermediate.
- **Nearest exemplar:** Things for the calm list–detail craft and forgiveness; BBEdit for the menu-bar completeness bar.
- **Character (signature elements):** (1) layout B: sidebar of Views / Playlists / Library, list, Inspector, bottom player bar with Episode over podcast over seek bar *(owner-fixed)*; (2) PodList blue #2b50c8 as the accent, with monogram artwork tiles tinted in it *(owner-fixed)*; (3) plain-spoken, capitalised domain vocabulary — Episode, Entry, Active Playlist, Current Entry, Clear on Played; (4) one reserved status row carrying a named Undo *(owner-fixed)*; (5) two-line title cells (Episode over podcast) with inline + and ▶.
- **Owner decisions (scored, never targeted):** layout B and the bottom player bar; system font with #2b50c8 accent in light and dark; Inspector Play alone and largest, five small icon actions below; Now Playing as a sheet; Delete Playlist with no question, "Playlist <name> moved to Trash" and Undo; two-click Unsubscribe that disarms after a few seconds; explicit Rename with Cancel and Save; unique, case-insensitive names; Entries / History / Listening Timeline as a segmented control; Rewind summary sheet; reserved status row; OPML in Settings and File; MCP off by default in Settings › Automation; Getting started until the first Subscription. Where an owner decision fails a check (APPEAR-02, WIN-05, CTRL-05), the failure is recorded and its cap stands, but it is not proposed as a target.

**Mismatches**
1. **Status row position** — top of the column on View, Playlist Entries, Search, Subscriptions; vertically centred on empty History and Trash (08, 14); bottom of the page on the Podcast page (18–21, `PodcastScreens.swift` 211).
2. **Retry wording** — "Try again" (sync row, Add podcasts, Podcast page) vs "Retry" (player bar, Now Playing) vs "Retry starting MCP", "Retry failed imports", "Retry bring-over".
3. **Error colour** — sync errors secondary grey (`MacWindow.swift` 370–373); every other error red; name-problem text in Playlist sheets grey caption.
4. **Sheet dismiss/confirm placement and weight** — Done top trailing in Now Playing; bottom trailing elsewhere; prominent default in Edit View and Subscription settings, plain in Playlist Settings and Add podcasts.
5. **Sheet title style** — bold `.title2` (Add podcasts, Edit View, Now Playing) vs regular `.title2` (Playlist Settings, New Playlist, Rewind Playlist, "Market Hours settings").
6. **Empty-state form** — `ContentUnavailableView` on History/Trash/Views/Search vs a one-line inline sentence for an empty Playlist and for Listening stats.
7. **Undo control** — status-row "Undo <name>" everywhere except the View editor's own "Undo edit".
8. **Capitalisation of the same command** — "Add podcasts" (sidebar, sheet title, empty states) vs "Add Podcasts…" (File menu); "Rename" (Playlist) vs "Rename…" (View).

## Checklist

**Coverage: 83.6 %** (pass 74 · fail 43 · N/O 23 · N/A 27; 167 IDs, 140 applicable). Core 79 IDs (37 pass, 19 fail, 13 N/O, 10 N/A); Mac 88 IDs (37 pass, 24 fail, 10 N/O, 17 N/A).

### Failures — blocker

- **[AX-01] VoiceOver names are wrong on the selection toolbar** — *where:* View/Search/Playlist toolbar when Episodes are selected (`light/03`, `07`, `33`). *Evidence (AX):* `03-episode-inspector.txt` 234–238: the five buttons for Play Now, Add to a Playlist, Download, Star and Mark Played are **all** exposed as `label="Play Now"`; only their tooltips differ, and the first two have none (C: `EpisodeEditingViews.swift` 55–75, unchanged since the capture). A VoiceOver user hears "Play Now" five times and cannot Star or Download from the toolbar. *Fix:* make each control its own toolbar item (or wrap the group in a `ControlGroup`) and give each an explicit `.accessibilityLabel` and `.help` ("Add selected Episodes to a Playlist", "Download selected Episodes", …); verify in Accessibility Inspector.
- **[MENU-02] Commands that exist only in the window** — *where:* menu bar (M) vs UI (C). *Evidence:* not in any menu: Subscribe / Unsubscribe, Subscription settings…, Share Podcast / Copy Feed Address, Edit Download Rules… (it is a context-menu command, `MacWindow.swift` 272–273), Restore Playlist (Trash rows), Rewind to Here (Timeline rows), Clear on Played, Show/Hide description, Episode detail. *Fix:* add a **Podcast** menu (Subscribe/Unsubscribe — the menu item can take the same two-step arming or simply rely on Undo —, Subscription Settings…, Edit Download Rules…, Share Podcast…, Copy Feed Address) and put Restore, Rewind to Here…, Clear on Played (checkmark) in the Playlist menu, each enabled from the focused selection.

### Failures — major

- **[APPEAR-04] Accent text fails contrast in Dark** — *where:* every accent-coloured label in Dark (`dark/07`, `dark/16`, `dark/01`). *Evidence (S, measured):* #2b50c8 on the dark backgrounds — sidebar "All Views"/"New View"/"New Playlist"/"Add podcasts" **2.1:1**, "Play this Playlist" **2.2:1**, player "Speed 1×"/"Sleep Off" **2.0:1**, Inspector action icons **2.0:1**, Edit View pop-up values and Rename…/Delete View/Cancel ≈2.2:1, "Current Entry" on the selected row **1.4:1**. Light passes (5.0–6.8:1). Also measured, approximate: secondary text on the Inspector's grey ("Not downloaded on this Device", "Its details and Playlists appear here.") 2.6:1 light / 2.9:1 dark. *Fix:* give `Color.podListAccent` a dark variant that keeps the hue but reaches ≥4.5:1 (e.g. #7D97FF ≈ 5.6:1 on #22262F) via an asset catalogue colour or `NSColor(name:dynamicProvider:)`; use `.secondary` only on the window background, not on the Inspector's tinted fill, or use `.primary` there.
- **[APPEAR-03] The accent has one value for every appearance** — *evidence (C):* `MacWindow.swift` 5–7, a fixed sRGB `Color` with no dark or Increase Contrast variant; `.tint(.podListAccent)` applies it to every control. *Fix:* as above, with an Increase Contrast variant (darker in light, lighter in dark) from the same dynamic provider.
- **[APPEAR-01] The Appearance override skips two windows** — *evidence (S, C):* with Appearance = Dark, Listening stats stays light (`dark/31` identical to `light/31`); `ListeningStatsScreen` and `PodListHelp` have no `.preferredColorScheme` (`PodListMac.swift` 35–37). *Fix:* apply the scheme once at app level (e.g. set `NSApp.appearance` from the setting) instead of per view.
- **[APPEAR-02] Controls and sidebar symbols ignore the user's accent colour** — owner decision (brand accent), recorded, not targeted. *Evidence (C):* `.tint(.podListAccent)` on the window root. Cap on Color stands.
- **[TYPE-04] Playback and row controls have glyph-sized hit regions** — *evidence (AX, C):* player Back 15 / Play / Forward 30 are 15×16, 11×12, 15×16 pt; row + and ▶ are 10×10 and 9×11 pt; Subscriptions' open chevron is 6×11 pt; all `.borderless` with no frame or `contentShape` (`PlayerViews.swift` 53–61, `MacWindow.swift` 348–351, 264–266). The Mac minimum is 20×20, default 28×28. *Fix:* `.frame(width: 28, height: 28).contentShape(Rectangle())` on each (the Inspector icons already do this).
- **[TYPE-05] Essential help clipped to one truncated line** — *evidence (S, AX):* Settings › Library: "Closing Add podcasts or this window does not stop the import. Progress stays in Library. Kee…" (`light/27`; AX value ends "Unfinishe"). *Fix:* `.fixedSize(horizontal: false, vertical: true)` on the paragraph, and move it under the Import OPML button it explains.
- **[SIDE-04] The sidebar loses its selection** — *evidence (S, C):* on All Views (`light/05`) and on a Podcast page (`light/18`) no sidebar row is highlighted, because All Views is a button and `.subscription(id)` has no row (`MacWindow.swift` 52–56). *Fix:* make All Views a real tagged row; keep Subscriptions highlighted while a Podcast page from it is shown.
- **[MENU-03] Menu items vanish instead of dimming; option menus have no checkmarks** — *evidence (C, M):* Episode › Play from Here / Move Up / Move Down / Remove from Playlist and Playlist › Play this Playlist / Playlist Settings… / Delete Playlist exist only while a Playlist is shown (`EpisodeEditingViews.swift` 162–183); with no window the Episode menu shrinks to one disabled "Play Now"; Speed, Sleep, chapters and Now Playing disappear with no player (`PlaybackCommands.swift` 33–40); Speed and Sleep items have no checkmark on the current value. *Fix:* always declare the items and use `.disabled(...)`; use `Picker` (inline) or `Toggle` inside the Speed/Sleep menus so the current choice is checked.
- **[EDIT-04] No Spelling, Substitutions, Transformations or Speech in Edit** — *evidence (M):* Edit ends at Search Library, AutoFill, Dictation, Emoji & Symbols, though the app has text fields and selectable show notes. *Fix:* add `TextEditingCommands()` to the scene's `.commands`.
- **[EDIT-02] Copy and Delete do nothing on selected Episodes or Entries** — *evidence (C, M):* no `onCopyCommand`/`onDeleteCommand`/`copyable` anywhere; Edit › Copy and Delete stay dimmed; removing an Entry is only Episode › Remove from Playlist (⌘⌫). *Fix:* `.copyable` (Episode title + share URL, as text and URL) on the tables; `.onDeleteCommand` on a Playlist's Entries table mapped to Remove from Playlist.
- **[TEXT-01] ⌘← and ⌘→ are taken from text fields** — *evidence (C):* Controls › Back 15 Seconds / Forward 30 Seconds use ⌘←/⌘→ as menu key equivalents, which AppKit matches before the field editor's line navigation; `PlaybackKeyboardGuard` protects only Space (`PlaybackCommands.swift` 25–26, 44–56). In Search or a name field, ⌘← skips audio instead of moving to the line start. *Fix:* extend the guard to ⌘←/⌘→ when an editable `NSTextView` is first responder, or move skip to ⌥⌘← / ⌥⌘→.
- **[KEY-03] Frequent Episode commands have no shortcuts** — *evidence (M, C):* Star, Mark Played, Download, Play Next, Play Last, Add to a Playlist…, Now Playing, Playlist Settings…, Edit View…, Previous/Next Chapter have none. *Fix:* assign ⌘-based shortcuts in the Episode and Controls menus, for example Return = Play Now (in lists), ⇧⌘→ Play Next, ⇧⌘↓ Play Last, ⇧⌘P Add to a Playlist…, ⌘L Star/Unstar, ⇧⌘U Mark Played/Unplayed, ⌥⌘D Download, ⇧⌘↩ Now Playing, ⌥⌘← / ⌥⌘→ Previous/Next Chapter; check each against the standard table before shipping.
- **[DLG-04] ⌘. stops playback instead of cancelling a sheet** — *evidence (C):* Controls › Stop is ⌘. app-wide (`PlaybackCommands.swift` 27); menu key equivalents win over the sheets' `.cancelAction`, so ⌘. in New Playlist, Edit View or Rewind stops the audio and leaves the sheet open. *Fix:* disable Stop while a sheet is key, or rebind Stop (Music's ⌘. is tolerable only because Music has almost no sheets).
- **[DLG-06] Sheets stack and outgrow the window** — *evidence (C, S):* Now Playing hosts its own `PlaylistSheetHost`, so Episode detail › + opens a sheet on a sheet (`PlayerViews.swift` 136, 177); Now Playing is fixed at 560×700 and Edit View at 600×730 against a 500 pt minimum window, and Edit View already hangs 22 pt below the 760 pt window (`light/16` is 1564 px tall). *Fix:* from Now Playing, close it and present Add to a Playlist on the window; let tall sheets size to content with `ScrollView` and a max height of the window's height minus margins.
- **[SET-03] Settings doesn't size to its pane** — *evidence (S, C, AX):* both panes are `.frame(width: 600, height: 690)`; Automation is three rows in a 778 pt window (`light/30`), and General hides "Server connection" below the fold (AX y 1026 in a window ending at 1030). *Fix:* drop the fixed height; let each pane take its ideal height (and scroll only past a screen-relative maximum).
- **[PTR-01] Sidebar objects have no context menu; podcast rows a thin one** — *evidence (C):* no `.contextMenu` on sidebar Views/Playlists/Subscriptions rows; Subscriptions rows offer only "Edit Download Rules…" (`MacWindow.swift` 272–273). *Fix:* Playlist row: Play, Playlist Settings…, Rename…, Clear on Played ✓, Delete Playlist; View row: Edit View…, Rename…, Duplicate, Delete View; Subscription row: Open, Unsubscribe, Subscription Settings…, Copy Feed Address, Share Podcast….
- **[DRAG-01] Drag and drop is reorder-only** — *evidence (C):* the only `draggable`/`dropDestination` is Entry reorder (`PlaylistContentView.swift` 57–60); Episodes can't be dragged onto a sidebar Playlist, a feed URL or OPML file can't be dropped on the window or Dock, nothing drags out. *Fix:* drop Episodes on sidebar Playlist rows (Play Last, undoable); accept `URL`/`.opml` drops on the window and register OPML as an importable type; drag out Episode share URLs.
- **[DRAG-04] The drag payload is private JSON as plain text** — *evidence (C):* `dragPayload` encodes `["playlistId","episodeId"…]` as a `String` (`PlaylistContentView.swift` 98–101); dropped into Notes or Mail it pastes that JSON. *Fix:* a `Transferable` type with a private UTType first and the Episode share URL / title as fallbacks.
- **[DATA-06] Nothing about the window's working context is restored** — *evidence (C):* `WindowModel.destination` always starts at `.subscriptions`; selection, sort order (reset on every navigation, `LibraryModel.swift` 233–236), sidebar visibility (`@State columns`), Playlist segment and scroll are not saved; no `SceneStorage`. *Fix:* `@SceneStorage` for destination, segment, sort and selection; persist column visibility.
- **[CTRL-03] Placeholder-only fields** — *evidence (S, C):* New Playlist ("Playlist name", `light/13`; its hint disappears once the name is valid) and Add podcasts ("Podcast name or feed address", `light/24`) lose their only label when typed in (`PlaylistSettingsViews.swift` 15, `PodcastScreens.swift` 30). *Fix:* a `LabeledContent("Name")`/`Form` row label, as Playlist Settings already does.
- **[CTRL-04] Numeric settings aren't locale-aware and are 410 pt wide** — *evidence (C, AX):* Skip intro/outro show `String(value)` ("0.0") and parse with `Double(text)`, so "1,5" fails in comma locales; the field spans 410 pt for a 1–3 digit value (`LibrarySettingsScreens.swift` 156–181; AX 28). *Fix:* `TextField(value:format: .number)` with `.frame(width: 60)` and a "sec" suffix, or a Stepper/pop-up like the Subscription override.
- **[COPY-02] View editor errors are far from, and don't name, their field** — *evidence (C):* "Enter a nonnegative number of minutes." appears at the sheet's bottom for either Minimum or Maximum length (`ViewEditorScreen.swift` 124, 155–159). *Fix:* show the message under the offending field, naming it.
- **[WIN-05] Transport controls live in a bottom bar** — owner decision (layout B), recorded, not targeted.
- **[SYS-05] No Spotlight** — *evidence (C):* no Core Spotlight indexing; `EpisodeEntity` isn't an `IndexedEntity`. *Fix:* adopt `IndexedEntity` for Episodes and Subscriptions so Spotlight and Siri find them.
- **[AX-03] No way to enlarge text** — *evidence (C):* no text-size setting or ⌘+/⌘− for show notes or lists. *Fix:* View › Bigger/Smaller (⌘+/⌘−) scaling list and Inspector fonts, persisted.

### What stands between this build and 9.0

1. **Blockers (cap two categories at 4.9):** AX-01 toolbar labels; MENU-02 menu coverage (Podcast menu, Restore, Rewind, Clear on Played, Edit Download Rules).
2. **Dark mode:** a dynamic accent with ≥4.5:1 in Dark and an Increase Contrast variant; Appearance applied app-wide. This alone lifts Color from Weak and raises the Overall cap.
3. **Mac behaviour gaps (major):** MENU-03 dim-not-hide and checkmarks; EDIT-02 Copy/Delete on rows; EDIT-04 `TextEditingCommands`; TEXT-01 ⌘←/⌘→; KEY-03 shortcuts; DLG-04 ⌘.; DATA-06 `SceneStorage`; PTR-01 sidebar context menus; DRAG-01/-04; SIDE-04; SET-03; SYS-05; AX-03.
