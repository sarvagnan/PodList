## Frame

- **Task:** keep a personal podcast queue: find Episodes through saved Views and Search, put them into Playlists, and listen, picking up where you left off on any Device.
- **Target:** web app (Android Chrome PWA, plus a Mac desktop window around the same build). Mac checks are N/A → target; the Mac shell is judged under Platform fit.
- **Posture:** sovereign on the desktop (an all-day listening companion with a persistent player); transient-to-sovereign on the phone (quick drops into Playlists and the player).
- **Audience:** perpetual intermediate; one owner who uses it daily.
- **Nearest exemplar:** Things (a calm personal tool: one list, its title, items, actions on hover or selection), with BBEdit's keyboard and menu completeness as the bar for the Mac shell.
- **Character:** (1) blue primary `#2b50c8` on a warm off-white page (`#f6f5f2`) (owner-fixed); (2) dark slate player bar and Now Playing drawer (owner-fixed); (3) Space Grotesk titles over Inter body (owner-fixed); (4) Views and Playlists as equal halves, navigated as lists, never capsules (owner-fixed, variant A); (5) coloured initials artwork tiles and round tinted Play buttons on every row.
- **Owner decisions:** light appearance only; layout variant A; Inter and Space Grotesk; blue primary; dark player bar; View editor R, Playlist settings and Rewind C, Library C. These are graded but not proposed for change.

## Flows

- **Tasks run (flow run r35):** browse (open a View, two Playlists, Library), listen (play, pause, skip, open another Playlist, play there), Now Playing (drawer, Up next, play from Up next, Episode page, close), fresh device (catalogue held 2 s), second tab. Widths: phone 412×915, zoomed desktop 720×450, desktop 1440×900. Light only (no dark theme exists).
- **Timed runs:** desktop, 5 runs after warm-up, darwin arm64, no throttling: open a Playlist p75 134 ms; Play from here p75 124 ms.
- **Script:** `packages/web/ui-flows/flows.mjs`; measurements `flows-r35/measurements.json`; strips `flows-r35/strips/<flow>/<width>/`; contact sheets `flows-r35/contact-*.png`.
- **Flow failures:** none (`flows-r35/summary.md`: "Stability failures: None").
- **Gap in the flow set:** the scripted flows don't cover the other repeated tasks: ticking rows for bulk actions, Add to a Playlist, editing a View, Search, Add podcasts. I probed those by hand (below); one of them moves the list under the pointer.
- **Additional probe (bulk selection, not in the flow run):** on `/views/new-this-week`, ticking the first row's box makes the bulk bar replace the toolbar picker. The bar is too wide to sit beside the title, so it wraps onto its own line and pushes the list down: the first tick box moves from y 144 to 191 at 1440×900 (+47 px) and from 165 to 251 at 720×450 (+86 px), and the h1 moves from y 35 to 32. A second click where row 2's box had been opened row 1's Episode at desktop width (URL became `?episode=ep_4410…`). At zoomed width the same click unticked row 1 and the bar went away. The person caused the move within 500 ms, so this isn't a FLOW-01 failure as written, but it's a layout jump that produces wrong actions; it's recorded as APPEAR-11 below (`p-space-check.png`, `p-tick-zoomed.png`, `t-tick.mjs` output).

**Mismatches**
1. **Selection marking:** View and Search mark the Episode open beside the list (3 px primary inset bar, accent tint, primary title, `.selected-row`). A Playlist with an Entry's detail open beside it marks nothing (Playlist vs View, Search). This is also SIDE-04.
2. **Empty-state form:** the dashed centred card with a title and one line (Trash, History, Search) vs a solid left-aligned card with an ellipsis title and a playful line (Listening stats) vs a bare muted sentence (Listening stats › Top podcasts).
3. **Section heading style:** small uppercase letterspaced labels (Settings, Episode detail, Subscription, View editor, sidebar) vs 18 px semibold sentence-case Inter h2 (Listening stats) vs Space Grotesk h2 (Refine, Edit View, Up next in).
4. **Where you are:** every screen marks one sidebar item or bottom tab, except Listening stats (no sidebar item, no tab) and the empty Trash on desktop (no sidebar item). The phone marks the Playlists tab for Trash. This is also LIST-06.

**Failures by severity**

*Major*
- **[SIDE-04]** The Playlist navigation pane doesn't highlight the Entry whose detail fills the adjacent panel. At `/playlists/default?episode=ep_66e9…` (1440×900, pointer away, focus cleared), the "What the Voyager records were for" row has the same classes as its unselected siblings (`aria-selected=null`, no `.selected-row`, no tint). Only the NOW row is tinted, so the eye pairs the panel with the wrong row (`p-pl-sel.png`; compare `p-view-ep-desktop.png` and `p-search-sel.png`, where the row is marked). **Fix:** in `routes/playlists/$playlistId.tsx`, pass `selected === entry.episodeId` into the Entry row and apply EpisodeRow's treatment: `selected-row shadow-[inset_3px_0_0_var(--color-primary)]`, primary title colour, and `aria-selected="true"` (or `aria-current="true"`). Because NOW already uses `bg-accent`, give the selected Entry `bg-secondary` plus the bar, so "playing" and "open" stay distinct when they're different rows. Keep the `.selected-row` forced-colours outline.
- **[LIST-06]** Navigation loses the current location on Listening stats. At `/listening-stats` the desktop sidebar has no current item (Settings, its parent per the "Settings ›" breadcrumb, is not marked), and on the phone **no** bottom tab is selected (`tour/desktop/06_listening-stats.png`, `tour/phone-b.png` panel 1). Every other screen marks exactly one, and Settings itself marks Library. Root cause: `BottomTabs.section()` tests `path.startsWith("/settings")`, but the route is `/listening-stats`. The sidebar Settings link uses exact route matching. **Fix:** treat `/listening-stats` as part of Settings: add it to the `/library` tab rule and give the sidebar Settings link the same held/active state there. (Optionally move the route to `/settings/listening-stats` so both match by prefix.) When the Trash is empty on desktop, either keep a Trash item in the sidebar while on `/playlists/trash` or mark the Playlists heading-link there, as on `/playlists`.

*Minor*
- **[APPEAR-11]** Ticking the first row on a View or Search swaps in a bulk bar that wraps below the title and pushes the list down 47 px (1440) or 86 px (720). The next click lands on the wrong row: it opened row 1's Episode at desktop width, and at zoomed width it unticked row 1 and the bar went away (evidence in Flows above). **Fix:** reserve the bar's space. Put the bulk bar in a fixed-height row under the title that always exists, holding the "Next and Last go to" picker and Edit View when nothing is ticked and the bulk actions when something is. Or overlay the bar on that row (absolutely positioned, the same 48 px height), so nothing below moves. Shorten it so it fits at 720: drop "in Default" from both buttons when the picker's value is shown in the count label ("1 selected · to Default").
- **[APPEAR-09]** The bulk bar's "New Playlist…" uses the `ListEnd` glyph, the same glyph as "Play Last" two buttons later (`components/episodes/BulkBar.tsx`), so one glyph carries two meanings in one toolbar. It also sits *before* the "1 selected" count, which reads as a stray first control. **Fix:** use `ListPlus` (as the sidebar + New Playlist does conceptually) or no icon, and move "New Playlist…" after Play Last (or into an "Add to…" item), so the bar reads count → Next → Last → Add → Mark Played → Star → Download → ×.

### What stands between this build and 9.0

1. **Clear the two major failures** (SIDE-04, LIST-06). They cap Platform fit at 6.9 and Overall at 8.9. With them fixed, Platform fit is about 8.8 and the Overall cap lifts to 10.
2. **Make the bulk bar hold still** (APPEAR-11) and tidy its order and glyph (APPEAR-09). This is the only place in the app where the person's next click misses.
3. **One empty-state form, one section-heading style.** Bring Listening stats onto the dashed centred empty card and the uppercase eyebrow labels, and onto the token scale (`text-row`/`text-title`, no `text-lg`/`text-xl`). Replace "Your stats start here… Every little listen counts." with the app's plain voice ("No listening yet. Play an Episode and it's counted here."). Hide the empty 30-day chart until there's a day to show.
4. **Separate "now playing" from "open"** with a second visual vocabulary (bar + neutral tint for open, blue tint for NOW). That keeps Color at Exemplary once selection is shown in Playlists.
5. **Craft nits that stop Exemplary:** the clipped "30" glyph at 200% text (size the skip number in px with the SVG, or scale the icon with it); the ragged right edge of the Subscription settings card; 13 px vs 16 px checkboxes; Listening Timeline logging an Undo as "added/moved" (log "You undid removing …" or fold it into the original line); link signifiers for the Settings "Listening stats" row (chevron) and the sidebar section labels; and extending the flow script to cover bulk ticking, Add to a Playlist, View editing, Search and Add podcasts, so the next regression of this kind is measured.
