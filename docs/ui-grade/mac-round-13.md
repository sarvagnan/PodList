## Frame

- **Task:** Find Episodes through saved Views (and Search or a podcast's page), queue them into ordered Playlists, and listen from the Active Playlist's player without losing your place.
- **Target:** Mac app (SwiftUI, macOS 26+; captures on macOS 27.2).
- **Posture:** Sovereign personal tool, used all day in the background with frequent short visits. Calm rather than dense.
- **Audience:** Perpetual intermediate; a podcast listener who builds a few Views and Playlists, then uses them daily.
- **Nearest exemplar:** Things (calm personal tool, complete menus, forgiving). Apple Podcasts and Music are the side-by-side platform reference.
- **Character:**
  1. Brand blue as the app's Multicolor AccentColor, with initials artwork tiles (DF, MH, SB) on pale brand tint (owner-fixed accent).
  2. Capitalized domain vocabulary (Episode, Playlist, Entry, Position, Device, View) and plain, consequence-focused copy ("Deleted Playlists can be restored from Trash for 30 days").
  3. Sidebar → Episode table → Inspector, with a floating glass player bar across the bottom (bottom placement owner-fixed).
  4. Views as saved filters with a one-line plain-language summary ("Published in the last 7 days · Unplayed · Newest Published first").
  5. Playlists with Entries / History / Listening Timeline, a marked Current Entry, and Rewind to Here.
- **Owner decisions (graded, not targets):** brand blue only via AccentColor (Multicolor); deep Dark accent `#3652D0`, including the Settings tab label macOS draws in it; bottom player bar; Light / Dark / follow macOS appearance choice; no customizable toolbar; two-click, self-disarming, undoable Unsubscribe; Liquid Glass on player bar, Inspector actions, Now Playing and sheet footers; Spotlight deferred; MCP off by default, App Intents on.

**Mismatches**
1. **Undo/feedback slot:** reserved on Playlists, inserted on every other list screen (Views, Search, Subscriptions, podcast page, Trash). The same message kind moves the content on some screens and not on others.
2. **Single closing button wording:** "Close" (Add Podcasts), "Done" (Now Playing), "Dismiss" (Getting Started). These are three words for the same act.
3. **Editing a name:** New Playlist and New View use a plain inline field (`13`, `17`). Playlist Settings and Edit View show static text plus a "Rename…" button that turns into a field with its own "Cancel Rename / Save Name" pair, above the sheet's own "Cancel / Save" (`11`, `12`, `16`). Two patterns for one field, and two commit levels in one sheet.
4. **List density:** All Views rows are 24 pt with no icon (`05.txt`: rows at y 281, 305, 329). Episode tables use 46–54 pt rows, Subscriptions 62 pt, and the Timeline 74.5 pt. All Views reads as a different control from its siblings.
5. **Date format:** "Nov 16, 2026 at 8:12 PM" in the Timeline (`09`) and "Nov 16 at 8:12 PM" in the Rewind sheet (`34`) for the same moment.
6. **Settings panes:** General is a grouped form with inset cards (`27`), while Automation is an ungrouped stack with a different label rhythm (`30`).
7. **Explanations shown twice:** Trash states the 30-day rule above the list and again in the empty state (`14`). Playlist Settings explains the buttons in body text (`11`).

### Failures by severity

**Blocker:** none.

**Major**
- **[WIN-05] The transport and now-playing information sit in a bottom bar** *(owner decision; graded, not a target)*. The player bar spans the window bottom at y 866–924 pt with Play, ±15/30 s, the scrubber, Speed, Sleep and the Active Playlist link (`01.txt` lines 89–100; `PlayerViews.swift:27`, `MacWindow.swift:220`). The HIG keeps bottom bars to limited status because windows are often dragged partly off-screen. Every control is also in the Controls menu with shortcuts, which softens the effect but doesn't change the placement. *Fix if ever revisited:* move transport into the toolbar's trailing or principal area, as Podcasts and Music do, and keep only position/status at the bottom.
- **[SYS-05] Episodes and podcasts are not indexed in Spotlight** *(owner decision: deferred)*. There is no `CSSearchableIndex`/`IndexedEntity` in `Sources/`. *Fix:* index Episodes and Subscriptions (title, podcast, published date, state) and route results to the Episode or podcast page.
- **[APPEAR-04] In Dark, the selected Settings pane label measures 1.7:1** *(owner-accepted consequence of the deep Dark accent)*. The "General"/"Automation" tab label is drawn `#435FDD` on `#484746` (`dark/27-settings-general-top.png`, `dark/30-settings-automation.png`), which is 1.73:1 against a 4.5:1 target for a control label. This is a recurring control label, so it is major under the scope rule, although the window title repeats the pane name. The app's own text passes everywhere measured (below). *Fix if revisited:* give the Dark Multicolor accent a lighter variant for system-drawn text (`#9DB1FF`, already the brand's Dark identity color, reaches 4.49:1 on `#484746`, and `#B2C1FF`, the Increase Contrast Dark brand color, reaches 5.28:1), or accept the cap.

**Minor**
- **[TOOL-03] The toolbar has five functional groups on Playlists and four on Views**, against an aim of at most three. On Playlists: Entries/History/Timeline segmented · {Play This Playlist, +, Settings} · {Play, Add, More} · Inspector · Search (`06`). On Views: {+, Edit} · {Play, Add, More} · Inspector · Search (`02`). `MacWindow.swift:167–203` keeps disabled selection controls in place to hold positions, a deliberate stability choice. *Fix:* fold "Play This Playlist" into the selection group's Play when nothing is selected, and move "+" (New Playlist/New View) out of the content toolbar, since the sidebar and File menu already have it. That leaves three groups plus Search.
- **[APPEAR-10] Secondary buttons in the glass sheet footers have no visible bezel.** "Cancel", "Delete Playlist" and "Delete View" fill `#FBFBFB` on a `#FDFDFD` footer (`light/11-playlist-settings.png`, crop at 1340–1420, 1090 px), about 1.01:1, so they read as plain text beside a filled Save. The cause is the footer's `.tint(.primary)` and glass background (`PodListSheet.swift:151`, `MacGlass.swift:40`). *Fix:* give secondary footer buttons `.buttonStyle(.bordered)` with the default tint, or drop the inner glass panel so the sheet's own background carries standard push buttons.
- **[COPY-01] "Show Description" names an action that doesn't happen.** The description is already shown, three lines max. The link appears even when the text fits, and toggling it to "Show Less" changes nothing visible on a one-line description (`18` vs `19`; the AX diff is only the label). Expanded is still capped at eight lines (`PodcastScreens.swift:133–134`). *Fix:* show "More"/"Less" only when the text is truncated (measure with `ViewThatFits` or a line-count check), and drop the 8-line cap when expanded.
- **[COPY-04] Tooltips are in title case, not sentence case:** "Play This Playlist", "New Playlist", "Playlist Settings (⌥⌘,)", "Back to Library (⌘[)", "Show or Hide Inspector (⌥⌘I)" (`MacWindow.swift:162–201`). *Fix:* "Play this playlist", "Back to library (⌘[)". Keep the capitalized domain nouns only if that is a deliberate glossary rule, and apply it to tooltips the same way it applies to body text.
- **[SYS-03] There is no URL scheme or deep link for Views, Playlists, podcast pages or Episodes in PodList.** `onOpenURL` accepts only `.opml` files (`PodListMac.swift:41–45`), and the bundle declares no `CFBundleURLTypes` (`scripts/build-mac-app.sh`). Share/Copy Link give publisher URLs, which don't reopen PodList's own objects. *Fix:* register `podlist://view/<id>`, `podlist://playlist/<id>`, `podlist://episode/<id>` and route them to `WindowModel.navigate`.
- **[SYS-06] There is no Share extension to send a podcast page or feed from Safari or other apps to Add Podcasts.** Dropping a feed URL on the window works (`LibraryDrops.swift`), but there is no receiving extension. *Fix:* add a Share extension that opens Add Podcasts with the shared URL prefilled.

## What stands between this build and 9.0

1. **The caps (owner decisions):** WIN-05 (bottom player bar) and SYS-05 (Spotlight) hold Platform fit at 6.9, and APPEAR-04 (Dark Settings tab label at 1.7:1) holds Color & contrast at 6.9. Overall can't reach 9.0 while any of them stands, because 9.0 needs every category at 7.0 or above. Spotlight is already planned. The other two are owner choices.
2. **Hierarchy and color restraint on the primary screen:** two accent-colored icon buttons on every Episode row, and four or five toolbar capsules.
3. **Cross-screen reconciliation:** one closing verb; one name-editing pattern (inline field, no nested Save); a reserved Undo slot on every list screen; one density for sibling lists; one date format; matching Settings pane styling.
4. **Sheet craft:** visible bezels for secondary footer buttons, less self-describing copy ("Save applies these settings…"), and no empty voids above footers.
5. **Verification gaps:** a measured flow run (FLOW-01–03, RESP-01/02, stability sub-score) at the minimum and half-screen widths, especially for the inserted Undo row.
