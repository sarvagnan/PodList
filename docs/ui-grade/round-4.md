## Frame

- **Task:** keep listening: pick Episodes from Views into Playlists, then play, pause, skip and work through a Playlist.
- **Target:** web app, an Android-first installable PWA; the macOS app is the same build in an Electron window (`packages/desktop/src/main.ts`), so Platform fit covers Android Chrome and a Mac desktop window. Mac checklist IDs: N/A → target.
- **Posture:** sovereign on desktop (left open all day); transient-to-sovereign on the phone (opened many times a day, briefly).
- **Audience:** perpetual intermediate, one owner who listens daily.
- **Nearest exemplar:** Things (calm personal list tool: one list, its title, its items, actions on hover or selection).
- **Character:** (1) warm off-white page `#f6f5f2` with one blue `#2b50c8` (owner); (2) Space Grotesk titles over Inter text (owner); (3) a dark navy player bar and drawer `#1b2a33` (owner); (4) Views and Playlists as equal halves, navigated by lists, never capsules (owner, variant A); (5) capitalised domain nouns used as a glossary (Episode, Entry, Playlist, View, Clear on Played) and plain status words (NOW, RESUME, Playing, Paused).
- **Owner decisions:** light only until a dark theme is designed; layout A; Inter + Space Grotesk; primary `#2b50c8`; dark player bar. Out of scope (absence not penalised): View editor, Search, Playlist settings and reordering, Rewind, Library management, Subscription settings, Settings screen, OPML, first run.

## Checklist

Coverage: **98.8%** (pass 73 · fail 10 · N/O 1 · N/A 21; 105 IDs, 84 applicable). Mac IDs: N/A → target.

**Failures by severity**

Major
- **[FLOW-04]** Undo out of view — the confirmation toast renders under the Now Playing drawer (z-30 vs z-50), so Remove from Up next, and Played / Star / Add on the drawer's Episode page, have no visible Undo, phone and desktop. *Fix:* raise `Toast.Viewport` above the drawer (`z-[60]`), make sure the modal drawer doesn't inert it, and while the drawer is open sit it at the drawer's bottom edge (`bottom: 1rem + safe-area`).
- **[FLOW-02]** Focus dropped to `<body>` when a keyboard action removes its own row (Remove Entry; Played in an Unplayed View). *Fix:* before the row leaves, move focus to the same cell of the next row (or previous, if it was last), as `rowGrid` already does for arrows; announce the toast politely as now.
- **[APPEAR-04]** Action labels fail contrast on the drawer's Episode page: "Downloaded" `#17181c` on `#1b2a33` = **1.20:1** (invisible, `shots/ph-drawer-episode-states.png`), pressed "Played" `#1d6b45` on navy = **2.27:1**, "Starred" `#b5791a` = **4.01:1**; and "Starred" on the light page `#b5791a` on `#f6f5f2` = **3.37:1** at 12 px. Cause: `EpisodeDetail` passes light-theme state colours (`text-foreground`, `text-positive`, `text-star`) to the label in both tones. *Fix:* give the tone table its own pressed colours (dark: `--player-foreground` label, state shown by a filled icon; light: a darker star, about `#8a5a0f`, 5.3:1), and keep colour on the icon, not the 12 px word.
- **[KEY-07]** Shift-Space toggles playback (`usePlayPauseKey` doesn't check `shiftKey`), so the browser's scroll-up key starts audio; it isn't in the shortcuts sheet. Space for Play/Pause is documented and passes. *Fix:* return early when `event.shiftKey`.
- **[WTYPE-04]** The desktop row tick box is 16 × 16 CSS px and sits on the row's stretched title link, so the 24 px spacing exception doesn't apply; a near miss opens the Episode instead of ticking. *Fix:* make the cell's hit area 24 × 24 or larger (a `label` or padded cell around the box, `size-6` minimum, 32 preferred).
- **[BROWSER-06]** Right-clicking an Episode row, which is entirely a link (title `::after` stretched over the row), shows the app's menu with no **Open in New Tab** or **Copy Link**, so the browser's link menu is lost; in the Mac app the same menu is HTML rather than a native menu. The menu does open from the ContextMenu key. *Fix:* add "Open in New Tab" and "Copy Link" to the row menu (web), and in Electron send the row's commands to a native `Menu.popup` through the preload.
- **[BROWSER-04]** The desktop "Next and Last go to" panel opens Episodes with `<button>`s that navigate (`TargetPanel.tsx` → `navigate({ search: { episode } })`), so ⌘-click, middle-click and Copy Link don't work there, unlike every other Episode title. *Fix:* render each Entry as a `Link` to `?episode=…` (or `/episodes/$id`) with the same plain-click interception the rows use.
- **[EDIT-05]** An Episode's URL can't be copied in the installed PWA or the Mac app (no address bar, no Copy Link in any menu), and ⌘/Ctrl-C on a focused or ticked Episode copies nothing. *Fix:* ⌘C on the grid copies "Title — Podcast" plus the Episode's link for each ticked or focused row; the BROWSER-06 menu item covers the pointer route.

Minor
- **[APPEAR-11]** On the drawer's Episode page, pressing Played on the current Episode of a Clear-on-Played Playlist advances playback and the page silently swaps to the next Episode, now showing "Played" unpressed — a contradictory state right after the press, with its Undo hidden (FLOW-04). *Fix:* keep the page on the Episode the person acted on (pin `episodeId` when the page opens), or return to the main page and say "Marked Played · Undo".
- **[APPEAR-09]** A check glyph means two things side by side: Played (`Check`) and Downloaded (`CircleCheck`) in the Episode action row and in the desktop row. *Fix:* Downloaded keeps the arrow-into-tray family (a filled `ArrowDownToLine` or `HardDriveDownload`); the check stays Played's alone.

**To reach 9.0.** Clearing the ten failures lifts the capped categories to their raw scores (~7.8–8.0) and Overall to about 7.8. The rest is craft no check names:
1. **Type scale:** collapse 13/14/15/16 px into two text steps (row 15, small 13), so a list title, a panel title and a sidebar item are told apart by weight and colour, not by one-pixel size changes.
2. **Now Playing as a first-class surface:** a designed dark tone for every reused component (action row, chapter list, toasts, menus), and Undo that lives inside the drawer.
3. **Desktop Playlist width:** cap the Entry list (~720 px) or give the Playlist the same right panel the View has (Up next, History), so the title and its controls aren't 1,000 px apart.
4. **Phone Playlist rows:** drop the number column on narrow widths (NOW/RESUME can move above the title, as at 22 rem already) and hide the empty track on unstarted Entries, giving titles one line more often.
5. **Iconography:** remove the repeated funnel and list tiles from the Views and Playlists lists (or make them carry information: a count, the active state), and use one glyph for Add.
6. **Fixed chrome at small heights:** at 720×450 the tabs and mini player take 31% of the height; slim the bar (one 56 px row) or hide the tab labels when the viewport is short.
7. **Mac window:** a native row context menu, the Edit › Undo label naming the action ("Undo Remove from Commute"), Reload moved out of View in the packaged app, and a minimum window that keeps the sidebar layout (or a 768 px minimum).
8. **Desktop player:** speed and sleep in the bar, as the layout doc specifies, so the drawer isn't needed for them.
