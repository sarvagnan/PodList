# Keep a permanent daily Listening log separate from the Listening Timeline

Listening stats need years of listening across the Library and all Devices. The Listening Timeline belongs to one Playlist, samples Positions for Rewind, and expires after 30 days; History records finished Entries only. Extending either would retain growing Episode-level documents without measuring listening time. Instead State has an optional Listening log, calendar day → Subscription id → seconds. Older States decode with no log and read as empty. SQLite persists it as a setting document. No older Timeline is backfilled because seeks and pauses cannot be reconstructed reliably.

Playback measures forward progress between Player Position events while playing, converts audio seconds by playback speed and caps it to elapsed wall time. Backward movement, jumps beyond elapsed time plus one second of event jitter, and gaps over 30 seconds count nothing. Starting, seeking, pausing and changing speed reset the sample boundary. Each existing ReportPosition operation optionally carries daily amounts accumulated since its preceding report. After the Played threshold, RecordListening carries the remaining listening without moving an Entry that was reset or cleared. The listening Device captures its local calendar date (splitting midnight with its UTC offset); replay never uses the server’s timezone. Like any sampled measurement, a crash can lose the unreported tail and a Player implementation that omits discontinuity events cannot distinguish a small external seek within the jitter tolerance from listening.

Replay adds amounts independently of Position conflict resolution or whether an Entry still exists. Operation-id deduplication (ADR 0003) prevents retries counting twice. Concurrent Devices contribute independently, including overlapping actual listening; no per-Device counters or permanent raw samples are retained. Rewind, Unplayed, Playlist deletion and Trash expiry leave time listened intact. At ten different podcasts per day, ten years require roughly 36,500 numeric totals plus 3,650 date keys: a few megabytes, independent of reporting frequency and Device count. Protocol version 4 defines this addition and includes a multi-Device conformance vector.

Episodes finished uses current EpisodeState Played facts with Playback source, for Episodes with a stored Episode row, grouped by Played date. It keeps counting podcasts the listener has unsubscribed from; View Library visibility does not restrict this count. Manual marks are excluded; Rewind or Unplayed can reduce the count. It is not a permanent count of completion events. Weeks start on Monday, and the current streak ends today or yesterday to allow listening later today. These meanings are stated on the page.
